# Obelisk branding

The icon and cover were generated with native OpenAI image generation for this project. They are original project artwork, not extracted from the UI references. The Windows ICO is a mechanical multi-size packaging of the generated PNG. No stock imagery or watermark removal was used.

**Direction:** flat monochrome, editorial spacing, architectural spire, thin orbital ellipses, small dots and straight connections. Inspired by the restrained presentation of Monolith, with an independent mark. Avoid glow, bevels, gradients, drop shadows and cyberpunk ornament.

**Icon generation prompt:** A near-black square with a centered white pointed obelisk, a narrow negative vertical cut, one thin elliptical orbit and two small orbital dots. Occupy about 65 percent of the composition. Crisp, minimal Swiss architectural identity, readable at app-icon sizes. No lettering, gradients, shadows, glow, bevels, 3D or watermark.

**Cover generation prompt:** Use the generated spire-and-orbit icon as the brand reference. Wide, flat monochrome editorial composition on off-white. Small icon at upper left, large lowercase wordmark “obelisk”. Exact headline “Your systems. In sync.” and subtitle “A private control plane for your home lab.” Footer “LIVE TOPOLOGY / LOCAL VOICE / AGENT VISIBILITY”. On the right, abstract thin concentric circles, dots and straight lines connecting three schematic storage, server and agent nodes. Generous negative space. No real IP addresses, telemetry, personal information, 3D, shadows, gradients or watermark.

Files: `docs/assets/obelisk-icon.png`, `docs/assets/obelisk-cover.png`, `tauri/src-tauri/icons/icon.ico`. The dashboard screenshot is captured from the synthetic browser tour, not generated artwork or a real installation.
